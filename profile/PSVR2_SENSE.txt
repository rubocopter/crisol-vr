Cross/Circle = Xbox A/B; Square/Triangle = X/Y. L1/R1 and L2/R2 use Sony
value sources through SteamVR's boolean threshold, not a nonexistent physical
click. Native sticks and their real clicks supply movement/turn and L3/R3.

hold more than 0.5 seconds View/Back, as implemented by UEVR. Verify both.
Create sends View/Back via a left-hand AButtonTouchRight marker and the
shipped input helper. Right Cross touch is ignored, including controller swap.
